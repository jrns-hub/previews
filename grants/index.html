GRANT GURU

Grants for Black Mountain and Swannanoa Valley nonprofits

A free readiness report for your organization, then help winning the grants that fit. By Wolfgang Joerns, Black Mountain.

What Grant Guru does for you

I find the grants that fit, keep the calendar so no deadline slips, write the applications with you, and file the reports funders ask for afterward. I served on a nonprofit board for a couple of years and have worked on several grant committees, so I know how stretched a small team gets.

FreeThis readiness report, and a 20 minute call or visit to go over it.
$150Walmart Spark Good setup: account, verification and your first local grant request, done together.
$300 to $800One full application, flat fee, depending on size. Most Black Mountain-Swannanoa Valley Endowment applications are at the low end.
$300 to $600 a monthThe grant pipeline: research, a shared deadline calendar, drafts, budgets and end-of-grant reports, all year.

Flat fees only. I never take a percentage of a grant: the grant professionals' code of ethics rules that out, and many funders do not allow grant money to pay the writer. You keep every dollar you win.
