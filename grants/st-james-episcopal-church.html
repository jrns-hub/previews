What to gather before applying

  • Your 501(c)(3) letter, or a fiscal sponsor's letter (churches can usually apply for community projects)
  • This year's budget and a one-page project budget
  • Your board list
  • Last year's financials or Form 990
  • How many people you served last year, and one short story
  • Two or three photos of your work
  • A Candid (GuideStar) profile, free and checked by many funders
