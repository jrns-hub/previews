A family farm that belongs to the community

Little Farm is a family and community farm stewarding a rich parcel of Swannanoa Valley floodplain farmland, through sustainable, resilient agricultural production and inclusive programming.

Run by Mary Bulan and family, on organic practices, with part of what we do going back to community organizations here in the valley.
