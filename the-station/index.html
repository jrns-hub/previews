Coffee by day. Cocktails by night.

A corner bar and coffee house at 100 Cherry Street in downtown Black Mountain. Bring food in from across the street, sit inside or out, and order the espresso martini.

The morning train

Weekend mornings it is a coffee house. Friendly, quick, reasonably priced, and not nearly as crowded as the places nearby. Kids love the hot chocolate.

The evening train

Then it turns into a wine, beer and spirits bar. A full bar, great beer choices, a happy hour, and bartenders people mention by name.

“After trying their espresso martini, I was convinced it was the best in North Carolina.”
Nicole Chandler, on Google

Rich espresso up front, and every other ingredient still comes through. Ask whoever is behind the bar.

Good to know

  • Bring your own foodGrab tacos or salads from across the street and eat at the bar.
  • Inside or outIndoor and outdoor seating on the corner of Cherry Street.
  • Happy hourWine on happy hour, per a recent visitor. Ask the bar for today’s times.
  • Coffee tooEspresso drinks and cold brew, opening at 10 AM Friday to Sunday.
